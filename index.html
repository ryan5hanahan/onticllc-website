Approach

Team

Sylvia Olveda

Co-Founder

  • BA in Psychology, Stanford University
  • Teaching Assistant to Philip Zimbardo
  • User experience researcher, leader, and strategist
  • 25+ years of experience

Sylvia earned her BA in psychology at Stanford, where she served as a Teaching Assistant for Philip Zimbardo. His course on the Psychology of Mind Control examined how cults, recruiters, advertisers, and institutions shape what people believe and do, often without them noticing. It changed how she saw influence: not as something that happens to the weak-willed, but as a set of techniques that work on almost everyone when the environment is built for them.

She wasn't just reading Zimbardo. She was inside the room, translating his research for students and working directly with his methods. That experience gave her a practitioner's understanding of how environments, not individual willpower, determine behavior.

She carried that lens into a career in user experience research, leadership, and strategy, where behavioral psychology meets product design. Sylvia has worked for Xbox, Microsoft Games, Amazon, Chase, Nordstrom, Alaska Airlines, and Target, among others, studying why people behave the way they do inside digital systems and how those systems should be built to serve them.

What she saw there wasn't always encouraging. The same principles Zimbardo taught (reward variability, social proof, loss aversion, identity-based compliance) were being built into interfaces not to help users, but to keep them. Not as a side effect. As the product.

Digital Balance is built on Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, a framework that treats compulsive behavior not as weakness but as a predictable response to a poorly designed environment. That framing is Sylvia's. When a platform is engineered to exploit your cognition, understanding your own mind matters more than trying to out-willpower it.

Ryan Shanahan

Co-Founder

  • Certified by ISC2, ISACA, and SANS/GIAC
  • Professional Member, Cognitive Security Institute
  • 25+ years of experience

Ryan is an information security practitioner and executive with multiple certifications and more than 25 years of experience protecting organizations from the ways technology can be turned against the people who use it. He has served as a CISO, built security operations programs from the ground up, and consulted for both Fortune 500 enterprises and early-stage companies across healthcare, commercial real estate, and technology services.

His path to Digital Balance started in adversarial security work: phishing simulations, social engineering assessments, incident response. He has served as a SANS teaching assistant for SEC542, Web Application Penetration Testing and Ethical Hacking, including a private session for NSA students. Years of studying how attackers exploit cognitive shortcuts led to an uncomfortable recognition. The same psychological levers used in targeted attacks (urgency, social proof, variable reward, loss aversion) were being deployed at population scale by the platforms billions of people use every day. The difference was legality, not impact.

Ryan is a professional member of the Cognitive Security Institute, a community of researchers and practitioners focused on defending human cognition against manipulation. He brings that security practitioner's lens to digital wellness, treating persuasive design as a threat model rather than a lifestyle problem, and built Digital Balance around the premise that users deserve to understand exactly what platforms are doing to hold their attention and why it works.
